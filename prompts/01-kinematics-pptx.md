# PROMPT 1

Please create a clean, modern 10-slide PowerPoint presentation (.pptx) on "Physics Mechanics 101: Introduction to 1D Kinematics".

To do this:
1. Install `python-pptx` if not already installed.
2. Write a Python script to generate the presentation with a modern 16:9 widescreen layout and high contrast design (e.g., dark navy header bars, clean white background, highlight cards for formulas).
3. Execute the script to produce `1D_Kinematics_Intro.pptx`.

Slide Breakdown (10 Slides):
Slide 1: Title Slide - Physics Mechanics 101: Intro to 1D Kinematics
Slide 2: What is Kinematics? (Definition, scalar vs vector, motion in 1D along a single axis)
Slide 3: Position & Displacement (Position x, Displacement Δx = x_f - x_i)
Slide 4: Distance vs. Displacement (Key comparison table with definitions & units)
Slide 5: Speed & Velocity (Average vs. Instantaneous, v_avg = Δx / Δt)
Slide 6: Acceleration (Definition, a_avg = Δv / Δt, units m/s^2, slowing down vs speeding up)
Slide 7: The Big 4 Kinematic Equations (Equations under constant acceleration)
Slide 8: Graphing Motion (Interpreting x-t, v-t, and a-t graphs + slopes/areas)
Slide 9: Free Fall & Gravity (Acceleration due to gravity g ≈ 9.8 m/s^2 down)
Slide 10: Problem-Solving Steps & Summary (Given, Unknown, Equation selection)

Design Rules:
- Widescreen 16:9 (`prs.slide_width = Inches(13.333)`, `prs.slide_height = Inches(7.5)`)
- Avoid dense paragraphs; use clear visual bullet points, key formula callout boxes, and bold key terms.
- Use readable fonts like Arial or Helvetica.
